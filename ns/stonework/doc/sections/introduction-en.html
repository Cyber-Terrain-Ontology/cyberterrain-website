Introduction back to ToC

STONEWORK is the ground-truth ontology for cyber threat intelligence in the CyberTerrain ecosystem. It models the full landscape analysts and AI systems reason over — threat actors, campaigns, malware, indicators, attack patterns, vulnerabilities, adversary techniques, software weaknesses, security controls, threat scenarios, and the risk they produce — as a single, formally structured OWL 2 model. STIX 2.1 is the OASIS standard most CTI platforms use to exchange threat data, but it is a data exchange format, not a reasoning substrate. Knowledge graphs, graph databases, and GraphRAG pipelines need data with formal, queryable structure — first-class relationships and reasoning across domains. STONEWORK provides that structure directly, as ground truth, rather than as a layer bolted onto an exchange format. STONEWORK aligns with established CTI framework ontologies as authoritative peer reference vocabularies rather than importing and extending them: STONES for STIX 2.1, MITRE ATT&CK for adversary techniques, CWE for software weaknesses, CAPEC for attack patterns, and CVE/CPE for vulnerabilities and products. Each framework retains its own independent versioning and identity; STONEWORK maps into them rather than absorbing them. The goal is to give analysts and AI-driven analysis pipelines a single, ground-truth semantic model of the cyber threat landscape that supports SPARQL queries, OWL reasoning, and integration across the CTI frameworks organizations already rely on.
