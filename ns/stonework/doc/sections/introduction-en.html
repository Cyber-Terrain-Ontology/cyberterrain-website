Introduction back to ToC

STONEWORK is a ground-truth ontology for cyber threat intelligence. It models the landscape analysts and AI systems reason over — threat actors, campaigns, malware, indicators, attack patterns, vulnerabilities, adversary techniques, software weaknesses, security controls, threat scenarios, and the risk they produce — as a single, formally structured OWL 2 model. STIX 2.1 is the OASIS standard most CTI platforms use to exchange threat data, but it is a data exchange format, not a reasoning substrate. Knowledge graphs, graph databases, and GraphRAG pipelines need data with formal, queryable structure — first-class relationships and reasoning across domains. STONEWORK provides that structure directly, as ground truth, rather than as a layer bolted onto an exchange format. STONEWORK aligns with established CTI frameworks as authoritative reference vocabularies rather than absorbing them. Each STONEWORK class captures the essence of a concept; the framework ontologies, shipped as separate modules, carry the framework-specific detail and are mapped to STONEWORK's classes. Modules cover STONES (STIX 2.1), MITRE ATT&CK, CAPEC, CWE, CVE, CVSS and CPE, NIST SP 800-53 and CIS Controls, MITRE D3FEND and EMB3D, the Cyber Kill Chain, OCSF, UCO, BFO 2020 and FATF illicit-finance typologies. Each framework keeps its own identity and versioning. The goal is to give analysts and AI-driven analysis pipelines a single, ground-truth semantic model of the cyber threat landscape that supports SPARQL queries, OWL reasoning, and integration across the CTI frameworks organizations already rely on.
