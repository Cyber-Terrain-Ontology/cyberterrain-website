Abstract

STONEWORK (Semantic Threat Ontology and Research Framework) is an OWL 2 ontology that serves as a ground-truth model for cyber threat intelligence. It provides an extensible foundation for modeling CTI concepts, objects, relationships, workflows and knowledge, and aligns with established CTI framework ontologies, including STONES (STIX 2.1), MITRE ATT&CK, CAPEC, CWE, CVE, CPE and NIST SP 800-53, as authoritative reference vocabularies.
